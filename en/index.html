THE METHOD

Rice, people, and all living things grow together in the paddy.

お米も人も生き物も みんな田んぼで育つ

一

Build the soil土づくり

Organic fertilizer (fermented chicken manure), decomposed straw, oyster shell and fossil shell return the minerals of mountain and sea to the paddy — the bounty of mountain and sea, in harmony.

二

Harness the microbes (BS)微生物（BS）を利用する

Natto bacillus, lactic acid bacteria, natural yeasts. The life within the soil grows the rice — tiny giants, unseen by the eye.

三

Care for the water水を守る

Drawing on the underground waters of the Omoi River, we grow without relying on pesticides or chemical fertilizers. The paddy is the matchmaker between mountain and sea.

四

Pass it forward未来へ繋げる

To school lunches, to homes, and overseas. Mineral-rich rice builds the body. Welcome to the world of planetary health.
